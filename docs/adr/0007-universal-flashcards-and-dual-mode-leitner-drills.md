# Universal Flashcards, Dual-Mode Leitner Drills, and Bidirectional Readiness Coupling

We store **Flashcards** (`front`, `back`, `box: 1..5`, `nextReviewAt`, `lastReviewedAt`, `reviewCount`, `lapseCount`, `lastRating`) in a top-level `flashcards` collection linked to any `noteId` (`plugins/flashcards-store.js` and `routes/flashcards/`), participating in workspace delta-sync (`GET /notes/sync`) and cascade deletion on Note or User removal. `GET /flashcards/drill` supports both `mode=scheduled` (`nextReviewAt <= now`) and `mode=cram_all` (ignoring `nextReviewAt` for last-minute interview or exam cramming) with `order=weakest_first | sequential | shuffled`, folder subtree rollup, and 1-hop external link expansion (`includeExternal: true`). Reviewing a card via `POST /flashcards/:id/review` (`rating: 'again' | 'hard' | 'good'`) advances or resets its 5-box Leitner schedule (`1d`, `3d`, `7d`, `14d`, `30d`, or `+1h` on `'again'`) and bidirectionally couples to the parent Note's `readiness`: promoting the parent Note to `'mastered'` when all of its flashcards reach `box >= 4`, promoting `'to_review'` to `'in_progress'` on first review, and demoting `'mastered'` to `'in_progress'` on `'again'` while bumping the Note's `revision` and emitting `NOTE_UPDATED`.
